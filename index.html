The Process

How It Works

From your ref sheet to your doorstep.

01
Submit your commission
Fill out the form with your fursona details, ref sheet, and what you want. Pay to lock in your slot.
02
I design it
I build your custom design based on your ref sheet and preferences. Takes 3–5 business days.
03
You approve it
I send you a mockup for review. Two rounds of revisions included free. You say yes, I print.
04
Ships to you
Printed and shipped directly to your door. Tracking number sent when it ships. Usually 5–7 days delivery.
