# Kokuro Customs — Business Summary

## What it is
Kokuro Customs is a made-to-order commission storefront for fully custom, fursona-themed apparel and accessories. Every item is designed around the customer's own fursona (colors, character, ref sheet) rather than sold as stock merch — one-of-a-kind pieces printed via sublimation/embroidery on demand. Ships internationally.

## How Commissions Work
1. **Browse & build cart** — customer picks items, sizes, and (for jerseys/bar tee) fills in custom design details.
2. **Submit & pay design deposit** — a non-refundable $25 deposit per distinct item locks the commission slot. Customer uploads a ref sheet and fursona details.
3. **Design phase** — once the deposit clears, a full mockup is sent for approval.
4. **Two free revision rounds** — included; additional revisions are $10 each. Nothing is printed until the customer approves the final mockup.
5. **Balance payment** — remaining balance is due only after mockup approval, then the order goes to print/fulfillment and ships directly to the customer.

## Payment & Fees
- **Deposit:** $25 per distinct item, non-refundable, due at submission.
- **Payment methods:** Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal for US (USD) orders. **International orders (EUR/CAD) are PayPal-only** — Venmo/Cash App are hidden since they aren't usable outside the US.
- **Balance:** calculated after tax, due after mockup approval, paid via the same methods.
- Customer enters their payment username/transaction ID at checkout so the deposit can be matched to their order.
- Order confirmation is emailed automatically (EmailJS) and captured via Formspree; a printable receipt is generated after submission.
