The Process

How This Goes Down

From your ref sheet to your doorstep. No surprises.

01
Browse & build your cart
Pick your items, choose sizes, and fill in jersey details. Add as many pieces as you want — your fursona details are collected once at checkout.
02
Submit & send $25 deposit
Upload your ref sheet, fill in your fursona details, and pay the $25 design deposit via Venmo (@kokurothewolf) or Cash App ($kokurothewolf) right at checkout. This locks your slot.
03
I design your piece
Once your deposit is confirmed I get to work. I'll send a full mockup within 3–5 business days via your preferred contact — email, Discord, TikTok, or Instagram DM.
04
Two rounds of revisions
Not feeling it? Tell me what to change. Two full revision rounds are included free. I won't move forward until you're happy with the design.
05
Approve & pay balance
Once you approve the final mockup, pay the remaining balance via Venmo or Cash App. Nothing goes to print until you've signed off and the balance is cleared.
06
Printed & shipped to you
Your piece ships directly from the print studio to your door. I'll send your tracking number the moment it ships — usually arrives in 5–7 days.
